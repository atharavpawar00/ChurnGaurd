    How ChurnGuard Works

    The model learns patterns from historical customer data and estimates the probability that a customer will churn. It does not claim to know the exact reason a specific customer would leave.

    Dataset

    IBM Telco Customer Churn dataset — 7,032 customers after cleaning, with demographic, service, and billing features.

    Preprocessing

    Categorical features are one-hot encoded. Numerical features (tenure, monthly charges, total charges) are standardised with StandardScaler.

    Model

    Logistic Regression with class_weight="balanced" to handle class imbalance. Trained with a stratified 80/20 split. random_state=42 ensures reproducibility.

    Prediction

    The model outputs a probability via predict_proba(). Risk thresholds: LOW <40%, MEDIUM 40–70%, HIGH ≥70%.
